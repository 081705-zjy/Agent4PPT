# Skill: Logic Review

## Role
Check narrative coherence and slide-to-slide progression.

## Inputs
- `story-strategy.md`
- `reuse-first-outline.json`
- `model-routed-plan.json`
- `manuscript.md`

## Outputs
- Logic section in `review-report.json`

## Rules
- Identify jumps, repeats, unsupported conclusions, and slides that do not serve
  the main story.
- Suggest rollback stage and affected slides when issues are blocking.
