# Skill: Duration And Density Review

## Role
Check whether slide count, text density, and notes match the requested duration.

## Inputs
- `question-state.json`
- `manuscript.md`
- `visual-plan.json`
- `render-log.md`

## Outputs
- Duration/density section in `review-report.json`

## Rules
- Flag slides with too much text or too little visible substance.
- Estimate whether the full deck fits the requested presentation time.
