# Skill: Audience Fit Review

## Role
Check whether the deck matches the target audience and presentation scenario.

## Inputs
- `question-state.json`
- `story-strategy.md`
- `manuscript.md`

## Outputs
- Audience-fit section in `review-report.json`

## Rules
- Flag sections that are too academic, too commercial, too shallow, or too detailed
  for the stated audience.
